Which runway direction are arrivals using?

Geneva Airport’s runway has two directions, 04 and 22. This tracker estimates each arrival’s landing direction from its reported heading. Treat the direction as a spotting guide: it is an estimate, and an aircraft may change course.

How current is the traffic?

Aircraft reports come from OpenSky, with route and aircraft details from ADSBdb. Positions may be estimated for up to 60 seconds between reports. Check the data status and last reported time above; the lowest reported arrival is ranked by altitude, so it may not be the next aircraft to land.
